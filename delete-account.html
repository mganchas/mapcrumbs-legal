Delete your account

MapCrumbs - Travel Diary · Developer: Michael Ganchas

This page is how you ask for your MapCrumbs - Travel Diary account and the personal data stored with it to be deleted. The developer is Michael Ganchas (Portugal). The Android package on Google Play is app.mapcrumbs.

How to delete the account

We could not confirm an in-app “delete account” screen from the app source or from the backend we inspected (there is no account-deletion function in the diary database). If your version of the app shows a delete control, use it. It should remove the same account data listed below. If you cannot find one, email us. Email is the path we can carry out.

  1. Send an email to mapcrumbs.contact@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my MapCrumbs account”.
  2. Send it from the email address on the account. If the account has no email (anonymous, or phone-only), include the user id shown in the app. If you use Sign in with Apple’s private relay, write from that relay address or include the user id.
  3. Say that you want the account and its data deleted. You do not need to explain why.

We may reply to confirm we have the right account. We then delete the data described below from active systems without undue delay, and we aim to finish within 30 days. You will get a short confirmation when it is done, unless we have no working email for that account.

If you never added an email, Google, Apple, or phone login

The app signs in anonymously on first launch so the diary can be saved. That session is still an account on our servers.

  • Uninstalling removes the app and the local copy from your device. It does not reliably delete diary data already saved under that anonymous user id. After the app is gone, we often cannot tell which id was yours.
  • To delete the server copy, email mapcrumbs.contact@gmail.com while you still have the app, and include the user id if the app shows it. If you already uninstalled and have no user id, email us with the device type and roughly when you used MapCrumbs. We will delete whatever we can still match to you.

What we delete

After a verified request, we delete:

  • the Firebase Authentication account: user id, and the email, phone number, Google profile name, and Google profile photo stored for that login
  • your cities, visits, Crumbs, notes, and categories you created
  • photos you uploaded and the photo records tied to your user id
  • traveller suggestions and other diary records stored for that user id
  • the push token used for notifications to that install, where we can match it
  • crash reports we can match to that user id

A link you already sent to someone else stays on their device. We cannot erase a copy they saved.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Hotel booking records. If you booked a stay, we keep the booking record (hotel, dates, status, price, and confirmation details) after the account is deleted. We need it to finish the stay, handle cancellation, refunds, and disputes, and to meet tax and accounting duties in Portugal. That period is normally up to 10 years. Guest name and email for the booking are handled by Nuitée Travel Limited (LiteAPI). Ask us if you also want those booking details limited to what the law still requires.
  • Backups. A hosting backup can hold a residual copy until that backup expires. We do not restore a deleted account from backup to keep using it.
  • Records we cannot tie to you. Aggregated counts, and crash reports with no user id, can remain. Catalogue content about a city (reference photos and city stories) is not your account and is not deleted.

Export before you delete

You can ask for a copy of your data first. Email mapcrumbs.contact@gmail.com with the subject “Export my MapCrumbs data”, from the account email or with your user id. The same contact handles both export and deletion. Details are in the privacy policy.
